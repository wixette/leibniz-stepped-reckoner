# Image provenance and licences

## Scans from *Zeitschrift für Vermessungswesen* 26 (1897): public domain

The images were rendered at 200 dpi from the Internet Archive scan https://archive.org/details/zeitschriftfrve10vermgoog. The authors died more than 70 years ago.

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `zfv1897_p289_jordan_fig1_gerke_drawing.png` | Jordan p. 289. Fig. 1 is Gerke's 1880 drawing, which has wrong sector-shaped windows. |
| `zfv1897_p290_jordan_fig2_geometric_drawing_67cm.png` | **Jordan Fig. 2: geometric drawing (length 67 cm) with Leibniz's Latin part names** (Pars immobilis/mobilis, Rotae minusculae, Magna Rota, Rota maiuscula, side crank K). It shows the 12-window *Brevis descriptio* version. |
| `zfv1897_p291_jordan_fig4_photo_april1892.png` | Pascal machine (Fig. 3) and **photo of the original, April 1892** (Fig. 4) |
| `zfv1897_p294_leupold_tab8_engraving.png` | Leupold 1727, Tab. VIII. Fig. I is the external perspective of Leibniz's machine. Figs. II–V show other instruments. |
| `zfv1897_p396_burkhardt_theory_drum_pitch.png` | Burkhardt p. 396: drum "Tempo" 22.5° |
| `zfv1897_p397_burkhardt_tempi_analysis.png` | Burkhardt p. 397: timing budget and the 72° pentagon carry |

## `commons/`: Wikimedia Commons

These are 1280 px downsampled copies. **The licence, author and source URL for each file are in `../data/wikimedia_commons_catalog.json`.** In summary:

- **CC BY-SA 4.0**: about 38 photos of the Lehmann/TU Dresden replica at Technische Sammlungen Dresden, by Dr. Bernd Gross, Plenuska, Eremeev, Wuselig, Jeremy Wolf and SchiDD. Attribution is required, and derivative works must use the same licence.
- **CC BY 3.0**: `Leibniz_Rechenmaschine_(1690).jpg` (the original, GWLB) and `Leibniz-Rechenmaschine_hg.jpg` (Hannes Grobe).
- **CC BY-SA 3.0 / 4.0**: `Leibnitzrechenmaschine.jpg` and `Staffelwalzeprinzipbha.jpg`
- **Public domain**: Meyers (1890s) drawings, the Turck photo, the Leupold engraving (Library of Congress scan), and Leibniz's own sketches

The Commons GIF animations were not downloaded: *Stepped drum mechanism.gif* is 62 MB. Their links are in the catalogue.
