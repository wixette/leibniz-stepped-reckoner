# Annotated bibliography: Leibniz stepped reckoner (Vier-Spezies-Rechenmaschine)

Collected 2026-10-17. Each entry gives a **tag** (used in the notes), its **relevance to 3D modelling**, and its **access status**.

Relevance: ★★★ = gives geometry/kinematics; ★★ = gives mechanism logic or reference images; ★ = context only.

Access: ✅ read in full and summarised here · 🟡 abstract or partial only · 🔒 not obtainable online (paywall, bot wall, archive or print).

## D. Searches that came up empty

- **No openly downloadable CAD/STL/glTF/Blender model** of the machine was found on Sketchfab, Thingiverse, GrabCAD, Printables or GitHub (2026-10-17), nor any **three.js/WebGL** interactive emulator. The only full CAD model (GWLB/mbe, above) was proprietary and is now offline. A blog-reported Blender work in progress ("Cameron / Open Development") could not be verified; the blog page now has no content.
