More pages

These pages show the mechanism step by step, in English. They were used to build and check the model, one page per milestone.

  • One digit: set a digit, turn the crank, watch the stepped drum, the result wheel and the carry store.
  • Three digits and the carry: 999 + 1 in ripple and historical carry modes, with the Zweihorn levers, carry stores and the drive chain.
  • The full machine: 16-digit register, 8-digit carriage with shifting, and the Magna Rota. Worked multiplication and division, and both readings of the carry.
