# v1.5.0 notes

The log of the next release, developed on the branch `dev-v1.5.0`: what was discussed, asked and decided, newest last. It continues [v1.4-notes.md](v1.4-notes.md); the release number is provisional.

## To ask Klaus Badur (kept up to date; drafted 2026-11-28)

What 1.4.0 built from his reply of 28 November is in v1.4-notes.md. Still open, and only he can answer:

1. **The Wagner protocol of 1706:** the two numbers, so that we can run the same multiplication here; the order of the multiplier's digits (units first, carriage moving left, or the highest first); and whether the 16-digit product was complete after the last turn, or needed idle turns with 0 on the knobs, or a shift back over a waiting carry. Ours, the *Original* carry: no idle turns in between; in random 8 × 8-digit products, units first, 30 % end with 1–2 idle turns to do, 3 % with a carry waiting in a store the carriage has already left, which only a shift back finishes.
2. **The detent pits and the catch:** their sizes (ours are estimates from the photos: a half-round pit 2.5 wide in a brass insert, a round head R 1.17, a spring plate 0.7 thick); and whether the original's catch can be pushed out of its pit by the lead screw, or only lifted by hand (ours rides out by itself: the owner's decision for the simulator).
3. **The nut:** how it engages the lead screw's cut groove (ours: a follower pin, 1.8 across, in the nut; no drawing shows it).
4. **His sheet 4,** missing from the set he sent (it may draw the catch, the rails or the nut).

**Not to ask** (settled; kept here so the list stays consistent):
- *Which bar the pentagons lie in front of and behind:* the base frame's rear bar, our beam c, already between the two rows (v1.4-notes, step 4).
- *The pentagons' height:* his answer of 28 November fixes the discs on the rear ends of the intermediate shafts, so their height is the shafts' (the register frame, his sheet 2): as built. Their 40 mm against the result discs' 35.5 mm is why they show above the register on the photos.
- *How his base frame sits against the carriage and the register in height:* his carriage frame on 25 mm rails puts the drum axes 37.5 above the frame's underside, ours 37.875 (v1.4-notes, step 4).
- *The register's cover* (none on the original) and *the detent springs' angle* (a few degrees): answered 28 November.
